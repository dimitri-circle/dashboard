Reactive Dot Ribbon

Preview the component, not the demo chrome.

The ribbon below is the embeddable custom element. It keeps its own styles in shadow DOM, streams to the right while in view, and reacts to mouse or touch without depending on the rest of the page.

Embed it like this

<reactive-dot-ribbon
  aria-label="Interactive halftone ribbon"
  hint="Move through the dots"
  source="./dots-pattern.webp"
  style="--dot-ribbon-aspect: 2048 / 1094;"
></reactive-dot-ribbon>

<script src="./script.js"></script>
